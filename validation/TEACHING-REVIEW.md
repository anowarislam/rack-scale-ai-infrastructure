# Teaching acceptance

The audience is new to rack-scale AI infrastructure. Some readers already operate Linux and distributed systems; the bridge supports missing prerequisites. The primary deliverable is clear theory with worked examples. A syllabus or collection of reference links does not satisfy this contract.

## A lesson must teach

1. **Purpose:** what problem the mechanism solves and why a simpler approach fails in a concrete case.
2. **Prerequisites:** what the reader needs to know, with links back to explanations already in the course.
3. **Mechanism:** a stepwise causal explanation using terms introduced before they become necessary.
4. **Examples:** at least two substantive worked cases where suitable, with inputs, assumptions, units, intermediate reasoning, and interpretation. Conceptual comparisons count when a numerical example would be artificial.
5. **Boundaries:** a changed assumption, counterexample, failure, or misconception that shows when the mental model stops being sufficient.
6. **Independent practice:** questions that require prediction or explanation, with separated answers explaining why the answer follows and why a tempting alternative fails.
7. **Application:** a bounded exercise that tests the theory, with expected evidence and a clear claim ceiling.

Neither word count nor the presence of these headings proves the lesson works. The reviewer must read the explanation and attempt the examples. An advanced topic may require a return to the bridge; it must not depend on an unexplained later chapter.

## Review procedure

Read the course entrypoint and follow a learner path without conversation context. Review a different author's work. Check the concept chain, attempt representative calculations independently, inspect answer keys, and identify any unsupported technical claim. Test whether changing an input changes the predicted result for an explained reason.

Report each issue with file, precise gap, consequence for understanding, confidence, and required correction. Block delivery for incorrect explanations, missing prerequisites that prevent progress, substantial coverage promised but absent, or an exercise whose answer cannot be derived from its data. Cosmetic preferences do not block delivery.

Record the scope of the review. A sample of chapters is a sample, not proof that every sentence was verified. Software tests and native-platform runs are separate evidence, recorded in [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md).

## Delivery check

The coordinator must resolve material findings before marking course authoring complete. Unrun physical labs remain explicitly unrun. The reader's mastery remains unassessed until the reader completes and defends the corresponding work.
