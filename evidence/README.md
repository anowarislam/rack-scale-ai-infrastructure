# Sources and evidence

Technical lessons cite their public sources near the relevant claims. The machine-readable ledgers divide source ownership across [systems](systems-sources.json), [platforms](platform-sources.json), and [fleet/practicum](fleet-sources.json). Each record contains an identifier, title, URL, access date, version or applicability scope, supported claims, and limitations.

A retrieved official document establishes only the claims it actually makes for its stated version and context. It does not prove that a local exercise ran successfully, that a product is installed here, or that a company deploys the documented feature internally. Execution evidence is recorded separately in [validation results](../validation/RESULTS.md).

Original course explanations, synthetic data, worked examples, and course rules are teaching material. Their numerical assumptions are stated in the exercise. They are not observations of a real fleet. A source reference does not turn a synthetic case into a production benchmark.

## Evidence labels

- **Verified public fact:** checked against the cited official source within its scope.
- **Observed execution:** a command or test was run in the recorded environment and its result retained.
- **Course design / synthetic:** an authored teaching choice or invented example, with assumptions exposed.
- **Inference:** an explanation supported by observations but still subject to alternatives.
- **Unknown / NOT_RUN:** insufficient evidence, inaccessible source, or an execution not performed.

Use these distinctions in your own portfolio. Preserve the failed attempt and the corrected result. Do not replace measured output with expected output from a lesson.

## Revalidation

Refresh the relevant official documents when a component version changes or before using a physical procedure. For native-platform or hardware sessions, record exact versions, image digests, topology, permissions, and recovery paths. Run the relevant software checks after editing a fixture, command, or answer key. Recheck cross-platform comparison claims when either scheduler changes.

The historical charter's NVIDIA job postings explain the original audience. This delivery does not assert those jobs remain open, that their contents are current, or that completing this material proves readiness for them. Private OpenAI, Anthropic, and NVIDIA architectures are outside the course's evidentiary claims.
