# Month 24: reproduce the work and defend the claim

**G4-E06: G4-O11 and G4-O12.** Reproduce the portfolio, answer challenges, close remediation, and define a bounded transfer plan. Completion means evidence supports named outcomes. It does not mean every hardware claim was attempted or that reading the course conferred a job qualification.

## A portfolio is an evidence chain

A reviewer should follow each claim to an observation, each observation to inputs and method, and each method to its limits. When the chain breaks, correct the claim or collect evidence. Confidence in the author cannot fill the gap.

**Worked example 1.** A report claims a 25% throughput gain: 80 useful units in 100s before and 100 in 100s afterward. The arithmetic is correct. But the after run used a different dataset. The supported result is two observed rates on two workloads; the causal effect remains unknown. Repeating matched workloads may repair the evidence. Adding the word approximately cannot.

**Worked example 2.** A state-machine change passes all local tests and is described as guaranteeing exactly-once rack updates. Tests covered single-process replay without hardware. The supported claim is the tested logical behavior. A broader claim needs durable state, crash testing, external-completion reconciliation, and supported integration. You need not build all of that to complete the bounded course outcome.

**Counterexample.** A learner actually ran authorized L2 recovery, recorded correct outputs, and met reset checks. State that bounded result plainly. Evidence discipline does not require calling completed work partial because a different fleet-scale claim remains unsupported.

## Build the evidence index

For each G1-G4 outcome, record status, fidelity, artifact, observed result, reviewer, and limitation. Consolidate repeated evidence rather than copying it. Preserve original failures beside remediated attempts. Separate software tests from learner assessment and course-observed leadership from workplace evidence.

Choose three claims for live defense: one mechanism, one quantitative conclusion, and one action/recovery or leadership decision. Your role-family capstone supplies the integrating narrative. A long document is not a substitute for finding those claims quickly.

## Ninety-minute defense

The reviewer has [a separate key](instructor/24-key.md). In the session:

1. Explain system and decision in ten minutes with one map and at most five evidence references.
2. Reproduce a quantitative result from immutable input; predict the output first.
3. Receive changed input or a fault-free control. Recalculate or revise diagnosis, then state whether the recommendation changes.
4. Demonstrate one recovered postcondition. For L2/L3, show the real execution record and explain what cannot safely be replayed live.
5. State the boundary of the claim and answer a viable alternative explanation.
6. Agree on precise remediation for any failing criterion.

Use [local tests](../labs/fleet/README.md) as relevant regression checks. They do not replace the defense or establish native infrastructure execution.

## Close remediation and transfer

Each remediation names the failed criterion, cause, smallest needed change/observation, and new evidence. Repeat affected checks and directly impacted invariants; retain the reviewer decision. A required hardware outcome unavailable at this time remains pending at that fidelity.

Write a 30/60/90-day transfer plan with one workplace experiment, a named or role-based owner/mentor review, and an observation window. For example: at 30 days determine whether local inventory authority is explicit; at 60 pilot one owner-approved read-only freshness check; at 90 review false alarms and decide whether to expand. This remains a transfer objective until the workplace work occurs.

## Evidence, abort, and acceptance

Submit the index, reproducible commands and hashes, reviewer notes, changed-input response, closed remediation, and transfer plan. Stop a replay that would exceed current real-system authorization. Archived evidence is preferable to re-enacting a physical operation for presentation.

Checks require exact reproduction when deterministic, justified tolerance otherwise, coherent causal reasoning, honest fidelity, and clear closure. Correct or withhold unsupported larger claims. A failed required outcome remains failed until its specific remediation passes.

## Reflection questions

1. Which claim did evidence overturn during the course?
2. Which calculation depends most on an unverified assumption?
3. What is the strongest completed result you can state within its explicit scope?
4. Which capability needs longitudinal or physical evidence, and how would you obtain it?

The key offers evaluation guidance, not fabricated personal answers.

## Four defense and transfer weeks

At 9h/week: week 1 indexes and rehearses; week 2 performs independent defense; week 3 closes bounded remediation; week 4 finalizes transfer and the outcome record. These complete the reserved weeks. Pending hardware evidence stays explicit.
