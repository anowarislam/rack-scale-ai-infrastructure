# Choose one role-family capstone

The capstone integrates evidence from months 19-24. It is part of G4-E05 and G4-E06, not an extra assessment or a second specialty. Select the role matching the kind of decision you want to practice; the course does not certify readiness for that job title.

| Role family | Packet | Accountable decision |
|---|---|---|
| Engineer or principal | [Technical intervention](engineer.md) | Where to enforce one invariant and how to prove recovery |
| Aspiring engineering leader | [Cross-team execution](aspiring-leader.md) | How to coordinate a readiness/recovery cycle to evidence-backed closure |
| Manager or director | [Portfolio decision](manager.md) | Which commitments and investments fit capacity, risk, and team constraints |

All choices retain one credible technical anchor from the specialty. Everyone explains the critical path, validates evidence, and understands recovery. The differences are scope of accountable decisions, stakeholder coordination, and the form of the final recommendation.

Use the changed evidence from [Month 23](../23-portfolio.md), not a static idealized story. Submit the initial decision, the revision, at least one completed local course action, one objectively closed finding, and residual risk. The worked fragments in each packet show the level of reasoning expected; they are not scripts to present as your own observations.

Use the common 0-3 rubric. Score each relevant dimension at least 2; do not average away failures. A technically impressive implementation cannot compensate for an unsupported causal claim. A polished leadership presentation cannot compensate for absent follow-through. Simulation establishes course-observed behavior only when someone observed it; sustained management remains workplace-evidenced or a transfer objective.
